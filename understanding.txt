ISO 55001 is an international standard that provides a step-by-step framework for organizations to manage their assets effectively. 
It helps companies get the most value out of their equipment, buildings, or finances, while balancing costs, risks, 
performance, and sustainability throughout their entire lifespan.

The Whole Lifecycle: It isn't just about maintaining things when they break. 
It governs an asset from its birth (planning and purchasing) to its operation, maintenance, and eventual disposal.

Why do companies use it?
Organizations adopt this standard to:Reduce operating costs by eliminating unnecessary expenses and reactive repairs.
Maximize the return on investment from everything they own.
Minimize risks like unexpected downtime or safety hazards
